How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • The sound of running water when no fixtures are in use
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
