Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
