Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Pontiac

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageUse your phone to record the damaged ceiling, attic and belongings first, hold off on throwing things out until that is done, and call us to get the drying started.
Insurance

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

When a leak happens suddenly and by accident, for example a branch going through the roof or wind stripping shingles, many homeowners policies pay for the water damage inside. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Common Causes

How It Usually Starts

  • Rubber boots around plumbing vent pipes that split after years in the sun.
  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Wind-torn, curling or split shingles that leave the underlayment open to rain.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Roof Framing

Wood in the roof structure that is soaked over and over darkens, swells and softens. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. It may take hold in 24 to 48 hours and work its way over the underside of the sheathing.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Leak water heads for openings in the ceiling, such as recessed lights, fan boxes and junction boxes. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Wall Cavities

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. Often the one clue is paint that blisters or trim that swells at the floor.
