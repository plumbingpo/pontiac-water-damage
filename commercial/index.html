Risk Assessment

Risk Assessments That Help Prevent Commercial Water Damage

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Sudden failures caused by old plumbing, poor waterproofing, or moisture nobody can see may lead to property damage, injuries, and expensive lawsuits. Pontiac Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. You receive a written report that lists each risk, ranks it by priority, and spells out the preventive steps to take.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
